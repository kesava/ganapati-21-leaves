Identification note. Ritual plant names can differ among regions and family traditions. గండకీ (Gaṇḍakī) is especially unsettled. This guide follows the Sterculia urens identification used by eFlora of India and marks it as uncertain.
For cultural and botanical education. Never taste a plant based on a photograph. Datura, oleander and giant milkweed are toxic. Follow each photograph link for its source and usage terms.
